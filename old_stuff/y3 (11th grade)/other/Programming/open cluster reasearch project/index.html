Controls:

Movement:

  • To move forward, press the "W" key
  • To move backward, press the "S" key
  • To move left, press the "A" key
  • To move right, press the "D" key
  • To move down, press the "SHIFT" key
  • To move up, press the "SPACE" key

To change the direction you are looking:

  1. Click the screen with your mouse cursor to "capture" the mouse. Now, you will look in the direction you move your mouse.
    Ex: If you move your mouse right, you will look more right, if you move your mouse up, you will look more up, ect.
  2. To use the keyboard to look around, use the Arrow keys:
    • To look up, press the "Arrow Up" key
    • To look down, press the "Arrow Down" key
    • To look right, press the "Arrow Right" key
    • To look left, press the "Arrow Left" key

Other useful keys:

  • To move faster, press the "C" key
  • To move slower, press the "X" key

Important Note:

Sometimes keys think they are still being pressed after you stop pressing them, causing you to keep moving in a certain way. To stop this, press the key again, and you should stop moving.
